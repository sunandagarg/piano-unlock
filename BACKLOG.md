# Backlog

The course is built in 30-day slices: build, practise with it for a month, review, then plan the next slice.

**Idea behind the course:** each week you play a piece, take it apart, and write your own with the same materials.

## Slice 1

Question to answer: does learning a piece and then writing with its materials, with the page listening, keep me practising for 30 days?

Setup: acoustic piano, iPhone or iPad on the music stand, site on GitHub Pages.

### Stage 3: composing every week
- [ ] Weekly loop: play it, take it apart, write your own
- [ ] Week 1: eight bars in G over I, IV, V, with "Amazing Grace" as the worked example
- [ ] Week 2: a verse over C, G, F with words (rule: repeat an idea, change its ending)
- [ ] Week 3: a slow phrase in 12/8 (rule: rise to one high point, then fall)
- [ ] Week 4: a chorus over the four chords (rule: a hook that keeps coming back)
- [ ] Recital: one learned song and one you wrote
- [ ] Composing tool: tap notes onto a staff, hear them over the chord plan, save
- [ ] Play to write: you play your tune and the page notates it
- [ ] Rule-checking feedback on your tune

### Stage 4: tempo and chord checks
- [ ] Tempo steadiness against the metronome
- [ ] Chord check, used to judge the 60-second unlock tests

## Draft: the level the full course aims for

A guess to revise after each slice. Roughly six 30-day stages, the first one free.

- **Play:** accompany yourself from a chord chart the same day; learn a short two-handed classical piece in a week or two.
- **Read:** both clefs, slowly, in keys up to two or three sharps or flats.
- **Hear:** recognise chords I, IV, V and vi; pick out a simple tune and find chords for it.
- **Create:** write and notate a 16-bar melody over your own progression; improvise for a minute.
- **Talk:** use the working vocabulary correctly.
- **Foundations:** relaxed hand, sensible fingering, clean pedal, knowing how to practise.
