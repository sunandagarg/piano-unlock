Rules, singing ladder, patterns and tricks

The three rules

  • Three coins. A passage is done when you play it cleanly three times in a row. A mistake puts you back to zero.
  • The floor. On a bad day, five minutes of replaying anything counts as done.
  • Never miss twice. The day after a skipped day is always at least a floor day.

Singing ladder

  1. Play the pattern until you can talk over it.
  2. Hum the tune over held chords.
  3. Speak the lyrics in rhythm over the full pattern.
  4. Sing. Where hands and voice collide, drop to held chords for that line.

Sheet music and recordings

Every Minuet note you need in week 1 is shown and played on this page. For the three pop songs the page teaches chords, patterns and tricks but does not reproduce the songs, so you need your own licensed chord chart or sheet and the recording on whichever service you use.

  • Minuet in G (Christian Petzold, from the Notebook for Anna Magdalena Bach) is in the public domain. Free scores are on IMSLP and Mutopia.
  • Drops of Jupiter on Musicnotes
  • This Year's Love on Musicnotes
  • Let It Be on Musicnotes
  • Love Song on Musicnotes

To record yourself, use your phone's voice memo app for now. A built-in recorder is planned.
